How to Play

💻 Desktop (PC)
  • Select a tile above and click on an empty space to place it.
  • Click on a tile on the board to swap it.
  • Hover and click the arrow to rotate.
  • Right-click to flip.
  • Double-click to remove.
📱 Mobile
  • Tap a tile above to select, tap an empty space to place.
  • Tap a tile on the board to rotate.
  • Tap and hold to flip.
  • Double-tap to remove.
